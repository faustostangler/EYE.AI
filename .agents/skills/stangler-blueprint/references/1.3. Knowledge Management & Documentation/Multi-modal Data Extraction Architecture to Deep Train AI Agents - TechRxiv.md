When utilizing scrapers to retrieve live content from the internet for AI applications, the integrity and accuracy of the data are compromised. This is primarily because the AI cannot perceive visual content such as images, graphs, and charts present in the source. This paper introduces a novel method for multi-modal data extraction and processing, enabling deep training of AI agents and models to enhance their accuracy.
