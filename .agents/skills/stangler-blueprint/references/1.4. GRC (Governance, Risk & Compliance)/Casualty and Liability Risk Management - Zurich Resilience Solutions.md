Frequently asked questions
What is casualty risk management?
It is the process of identifying and reducing liability risks that could lead to injury, property damage or third-party financial loss.
What are workplace safety risks?
These are risks like slips, falls, ergonomic strain or exposure to violence that can occur in the workplace and impact employee safety.
What is a workplace risk assessment?
It's a structured evaluation of operations to identify hazards, assess risks and implement controls that improve safety and compliance.
How does Zurich Resilience Solutions support work health and safety?
We offer expert-led assessments, custom training and consulting to improve safety practices and reduce liability exposures.
Is casualty risk management the same as insurance?
No. While insurance transfers financial risk, Zurich Resilience Solutions focuses on reducing the likelihood and impact of incidents before they happen.
