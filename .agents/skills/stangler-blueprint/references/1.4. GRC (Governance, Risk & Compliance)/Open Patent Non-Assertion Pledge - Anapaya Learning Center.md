Open Patent Non-Assertion Pledge
Anapaya Systems AG is committed to promoting innovation to further the overall growth and advancement of information technology and believes that Free or Open Source Software is a very important tool for fostering innovation. Anapaya Systems AG is therefore pledging the free use of certain of its patents in connection with Free or Open Source Software on the following terms:
Definitions 
“Free or Open Source Software” means any software that is licensed or otherwise distributed to the public in such a way that satisfies any version of “The Open Source Definition” provided by the Open Source Initiative at opensource.org/osd or any version of “The Free Software Definition” provided by the Free Software Foundation at gnu.org/philosophy/free-sw.html.
“Pledged Patents” means the specific patents listed by Anapaya Systems AG here. Anapaya Systems AG may supplement this list of patents from time to time in its discretion.
“Pledge” means the promise set forth in the first two paragraphs under “Our Pledge.”
Our Pledge 
Anapaya Systems AG promises to each person or entity that develops, distributes or uses Free or Open Source Software (a “Pledge Recipient”) that Anapaya Systems AG will not bring a lawsuit or other legal proceeding against a Pledge Recipient for patent infringement under any Pledged Patents based on the Pledge Recipient's (i) development, manufacture, use, sale, offer for sale, lease, license, exportation, importation or distribution of any Free or Open Source Software, or (ii) internal-only use of Free or Open Source Software, either as obtained by Pledge Recipient or as modified by Pledge Recipient, in standalone form or combined with hardware or with any other software (“Internal-Only Use”). The preceding Pledge does not apply to any infringement of the Pledged Patents by hardware or by software that is not Free or Open Source Software, or by Free or Open Source Software combined with special purpose hardware or with software that is not Free or Open Source Software (except Internal-Only Use).
It is Anapaya Systems AG's intent that the Pledge be legally binding, irrevocable (except as otherwise provided under “Defensive Termination” below) and enforceable against Anapaya Systems AG and entities controlled by Anapaya Systems AG, and their successors and assigns. Thus, Anapaya Systems AG will require any person or entity to whom it sells or transfers any of the Pledged Patents to agree, in writing, to abide by the Pledge and to place a similar requirement on any subsequent transferees to do the same.
The Pledge is not an assurance that any of the Pledged Patents cover any particular software or hardware or are enforceable, that the Pledged Patents are all patents that do or may cover any particular Free or Open Source Software, that any activities covered by the Pledge will not infringe patents or other intellectual property rights of a third party, or that Anapaya Systems AG will add any other patents to the list of Pledged Patents. Except as expressly stated in the Pledge, no other rights are waived or granted by Anapaya Systems AG or received by a Pledge Recipient, whether by implication, estoppel, or otherwise.
Defensive Termination 
Because our Pledge is a promise not to assert certain Anapaya Systems AG patents without requiring any payment from a Pledge Recipient, we think it is only fair that we condition the Pledge upon the Pledge Recipient (and its affiliates) not asserting or profiting from the assertion of patents against Anapaya Systems AG, its affiliates, or its products or services. Accordingly, Anapaya Systems AG reserves the right to terminate the Pledge, to the extent Anapaya Systems AG deems necessary to protect itself, its affiliates, or its products and services (“Defensive Termination”) with respect to any Pledge Recipient (or affiliate) who files a lawsuit or other legal proceeding for patent infringement or who has a direct financial interest in such lawsuit or other legal proceeding (an “Asserting Party”) against Anapaya Systems AG or any entity controlled by Anapaya Systems AG or against any third party based in whole or in part on any product or service developed by or on behalf of Anapaya Systems AG or any entity controlled by Anapaya Systems AG. Any Defensive Termination by Anapaya Systems AG with respect to an Asserting Party shall have the same effect as if Our Pledge was never extended to such Asserting Party in the first instance. Anapaya Systems AG, in its sole discretion, shall determine the manner and terms, if any, by which rights under Pledged Patents may be extended to an Asserting Party after that Asserting Party's lawsuit or other legal proceeding is permanently dismissed, terminated or withdrawn in writing.
Mistaken Assertion 
Should Anapaya Systems AG ever initiate a lawsuit or other legal proceeding for patent infringement based on software which is not the subject of a Defensive Termination, and then receive written notice from the party against whom such lawsuit or other legal proceeding has been filed providing sufficient information for Anapaya Systems AG to reasonably determine that such software in fact satisfies the requirements of the Pledge, then Anapaya Systems AG will use reasonable efforts to withdraw such lawsuit (or the applicable claims therein) or move to terminate such other legal proceeding (or the applicable portions thereof) within sixty (60) days after receiving such written notice.
