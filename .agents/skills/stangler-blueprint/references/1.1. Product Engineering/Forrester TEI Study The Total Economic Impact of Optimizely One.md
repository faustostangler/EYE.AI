FAQ
What is a Forrester Total Economic Impact (TEI) study?
The Forrester Total Economic Impact™ (TEI) study is an independent analysis, this one commissioned by Optimizely, that quantifies the costs, benefits, risks, and ROI of a technology investment. It provides a standardized financial framework — including ROI, net present value (NPV), and payback period, to help organizations make informed purchasing decisions.
How is ROI calculated in a TEI study?
ROI in a TEI study is calculated by comparing the quantified benefits (e.g., revenue uplift,
productivity gains, cost savings) against the total costs (e.g., licensing, implementation, training). For Optimizely One, the benefits significantly outweighed the costs, producing a three-year 446% return.
Who should read the Forrester TEI of Optimizely One?
This study is designed for CMOs, marketing leaders, digital and web leaders, and IT decision-makers who need a proven financial business case for investing in Optimizely One.
How can this TEI study help me build a business case?
The TEI provides third-party, verified metrics on ROI, cost savings, and productivity improvements. You can use the study to:
Justify marketing technology investments with your CFO or procurement teams
Benchmark potential ROI against peer organizations
Understand total cost of ownership (TCO) and payback timelines
