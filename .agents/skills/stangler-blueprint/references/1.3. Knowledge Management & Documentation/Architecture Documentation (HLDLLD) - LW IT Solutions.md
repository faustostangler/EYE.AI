When architecture is not documented, delivery teams make assumptions in isolation: solutions drift, security decisions are inconsistent, and stakeholders only discover gaps late in the project - when remediation is expensive. High‑Level Design (HLD) aligns stakeholders on the target architecture and the 'why' behind key decisions. Low‑Level Design (LLD) translates those decisions into implementable detail: configuration approach, integrations, dependencies, and operational controls. For Microsoft 365, Azure, and security platforms, strong design documentation is often the difference between a predictable deployment and a programme defined by rework and escalations. LW IT Solutions delivers Architecture Documentation (HLD/LLD) as a structured service to create implementation-ready design packs. We capture requirements and constraints, document current and target states, define architecture patterns (identity, networking, data flows, and security controls as applicable), and record key design decisions and assumptions. You receive a clear HLD/LLD set that supports build, assurance, and handover - plus a roadmap for implementation and validation so delivery teams can execute with confidence. Talk through your requirements and leave with a clear next-step plan. Frequently Asked Questions What is the difference between HLD and LLD? HLD describes the overall solution architecture, major components, and the rationale for decisions. LLD provides implementation-ready detail: configurations, integrations, dependencies, and operational considerations within scope. What platforms can you produce HLD/LLD for? We produce design documentation for Microsoft 365, Azure, security platforms, data/analytics services, and related integrations—aligned to the scope agreed in discovery. Do you include security and identity design? Yes, where relevant. We document identity, access, and security controls appropriate to the solution and your governance requirements. Will the design include a build plan? We provide an implementation roadmap and sequencing guidance. Full project plans are typically delivered under the project/programme delivery services. Can you work with our internal architects and engineers? Yes. We collaborate with your teams to capture constraints and validate decisions, ensuring the outputs fit your organisation's standards and operating model. Do you document trade-offs and rationale? Yes. We record key decisions, options considered, and assumptions so design choices remain defensible and traceable. Can this support partner or multi-vendor delivery? Yes. Clear HLD/LLD reduces ambiguity and is often essential for effective partner delivery and governance. How detailed is the LLD? LLD detail is scoped to what is required for implementable delivery. We agree boundaries up front to avoid over-documenting or missing critical configuration detail. Will you provide diagrams I can reuse on the website or in governance packs? We provide diagrams suitable for project delivery and governance. Reuse for marketing materials is possible where appropriate and agreed. Can this be fixed price? Yes, for a defined platform and scope boundary. Larger multi-workstream architecture packs are usually delivered in phases. Get an expert-led assessment with a prioritised remediation backlog.
