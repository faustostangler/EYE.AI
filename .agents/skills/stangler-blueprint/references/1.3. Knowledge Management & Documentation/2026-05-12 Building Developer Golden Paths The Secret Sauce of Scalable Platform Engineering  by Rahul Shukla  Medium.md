What Exactly is a Golden Path? A Golden Path is: 👉 “The paved road that enables developers to achieve 80% of common tasks in the easiest, fastest, and most secure way possible.” Unlike rigid rules, Golden Paths offer guardrails, not handcuffs. Developers can take detours if needed, but the path ensures: ✅ Self-service scaffolding (no tickets to infra teams). ✅ Opinionated defaults (CI/CD, IaC, monitoring baked in). ✅ Security & compliance by default. ✅ Consistency across teams and services. Think of it like Express Lanes on a highway — fast, safe, and predictable. 🏗️ Blueprint of a Golden Path A robust Golden Path usually consists of: Service Templates (code scaffolds with pipelines) Infrastructure Blueprints (Terraform/Pulumi modules) Service Catalog / Portal (discovery + one-click usage) Guardrails & Policies (linting, OPA/Kyverno, scanners) Continuous Maintenance (versioning, updates, support)
