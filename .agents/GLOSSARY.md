# Ubiquitous Language Glossary

This document serves as the Single Source of Truth for the Domain's Ubiquitous Language, as required by our DDD and Clean Architecture standards. It eliminates translation errors between business experts and engineers.

## 1. Clinical Core Subdomain (The Business Domain)
Terms representing medical language and hospital business rules.

*   **PEP (Prontuário Eletrônico do Paciente):** The legacy external system. Acts as the **Upstream System** that triggers ingestion events for our architecture.
*   **ClinicalRecord (Aggregate Root):** Our purified internal representation of patient data. Unlike the PEP Payload (which is dirty and structured for the external system), the `ClinicalRecord` is validated and typed.
*   **MedicalGuideline (Entity):** Official medical protocols, articles, and guidelines from the hospital that have been vectorized in Qdrant. It is the clinical "source of truth."
*   **ClinicalConduct (Value Object/Output):** The structured (JSON) result of the analysis generated by the model. Represents the medical recommendation based on the `ClinicalRecord` and `MedicalGuidelines`.

## 2. Security & Compliance Subdomain (The LGPD Boundary)
Terms dedicated to data protection and system isolation.

*   **PHI / PII (Protected Health Information / Personally Identifiable Information):** Any sensitive data (name, CPF, exact age, address) that can identify the patient.
*   **PII Scrubber:** Domain component in the **Anti-Corruption Layer (ACL)** responsible for applying regular expressions and fast NER (Named Entity Recognition) models to anonymize PHI/PII before data touches Redis, the LLM, or Loki logs.
*   **Anti-Corruption Layer (ACL):** The hexagonal barrier (Ports & Adapters) that translates the PEP Payload into a `ClinicalRecord` and applies the `PII Scrubber`, ensuring external system "noise" does not pollute the Core Domain.

## 3. Inference & Async Execution Subdomain (The Thermal Engine)
Terms dealing with physical hardware constraints (6GB VRAM) and RAG resilience.

*   **InferenceWorker:** The daemon process (isolated from the REST API) that consumes messages from the queue (Redis) one at a time. Ensures GPU concurrency is always = 1 to prevent Out of Memory (OOM).
*   **KnowledgeRuntime:** The RAG orchestrator engine. It encompasses semantic search in Qdrant and final prompt assembly before sending it to the LLM.
*   **KVCacheLimit:** The strict context token ceiling configured in `llama.cpp` to ensure LLM processing never exceeds the 6GB VRAM of the RTX 2060.
*   **VectorID / TraceID:** Telemetry identifiers. The `TraceID` follows the request from the API, while the `VectorID` identifies which piece of the `MedicalGuideline` was injected into the prompt, ensuring explainability of the AI decision without leaking PHI.


## 4. Visio-Chat Hermes (Clinical Preceptorship)
Terms specific to the clinical decision support MVP.

*   **MedicalPreceptor:** The clinical mentor AI persona designed to guide medical decisions safely without external hallucinations.

## 5. Developer Experience & Platform (The Factory)
Terms for repository organization, isolation, and engineering culture.

*   **Playground:** A root-level directory for spikes, experimental scratchpads, and rapid prototyping that do not follow TDD/Hexagonal standards.
*   **Dark Folder:** A directory structure (like `playground/`) that is explicitly excluded from the domain model, ADR requirements, and production import graphs to allow for high-velocity exploration.
