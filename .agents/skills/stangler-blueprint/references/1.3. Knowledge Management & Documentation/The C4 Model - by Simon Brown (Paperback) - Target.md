The C4 Model: Visualizing Software Architecture is a practical guide to creating diagrams that help teams understand, build, and talk about software systems more effectively. Developed by Simon Brown and used by thousands of teams worldwide, the C4 model breaks architecture down into four simple levels (context, container, component, and code), making it easier to see the big picture and the details. Whether you're onboarding a new teammate, presenting to stakeholders, or just trying to make sense of a complex system, this book shows you how to create visuals that actually make an impact. With approachable guidance, real examples, and tips for tailoring diagrams to different audiences, it's a must-have for any developer or architect who wants to make their ideas easier to understand.
