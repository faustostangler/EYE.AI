Asset Inventory
What We Do: IT Asset Management
Know What You Own. Control What You Use.
The foundation of every effective ITAM, SAM, CMDB, and FinOps decision is a reliable Asset Inventory.
Through a flexible set of technology and expertise, InSequence delivers visibility into hardware, software, and cloud inventory.
Inventory Done Right Is More Than a List. It is Your Source of Truth.
Asset inventory isn't just an internal report—it's the backbone of security audits, cost allocations, vendor negotiations, cloud migrations, and compliance strategies. Yet many organizations struggle with gaps in coverage, duplicate entries, stale records, incomplete lifecycle data, or siloed sources across platforms.
Our
** Asset Inventory** services unify your inventory into a single source of truth across hardware, software, SaaS, and Cloud — structured, normalized, and ready to fuel your business goals.
Hardware & Software Inventory Normalization Remove duplicates, standardize models, and align asset data to authoritative sources.
Cloud & SaaS Visibility Extend inventory beyond on-prem to include cloud infrastructure and SaaS applications.
Lifecycle Mapping Track from procurement to retirement with key metadata: ownership, location, contract linkage, warranty, and support data.
CMDB Enrichment Sync inventory with configuration data and dependencies to ensure your CMDB reflects reality.
Discovery & Reconciliation Automation Leverage tool integrations and curated playbooks to reconcile scan data with procurement and deployment records.
Tagging & Attribution Standards Establish governance for cost center attribution, business service mapping, and end-user accountability.
