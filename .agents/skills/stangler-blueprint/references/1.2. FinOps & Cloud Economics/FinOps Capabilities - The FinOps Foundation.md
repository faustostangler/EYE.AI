Allocation
Define strategies to assign and share technology costs using accounts, tags, labels, and other metadata, creating accountability among teams and projects within an organization. Domain: Understand Usage & Cost](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/allocation/) 2. [
Anomaly Management
Anomaly Management gives a FinOps team the ability to detect, identify, clarify, alert on, and manage unexpected technology cost events in a timely manner, in order to minimize impact to the business. Domain: Understand Usage & Cost](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/anomaly-management/) 3. [
Architecting & Workload Placement
With the introduction of FinOps and broader use of technology, engineering and product teams not only have the responsibility to choose services that meet the operational requirements of their systems, but also the sustainability and financial viability of those systems. Domain: Optimize Usage & Cost](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/architecting-workload-placement/) 4. [
Automation, Tools, & Services
A FinOps Automation, Tools, & Services refer to software with features or functionality that enables any of the FinOps Capabilities. Domain: Manage the FinOps Practice](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/automation-tools-services/) 5. [
Budgeting
Strategic and ongoing process for setting limits, monitoring, and managing technology spending, aligned with business objectives, to ensure accountability and predictable financial outcomes for an organization's systems. Domain: Quantify Business Value](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/budgeting/) 6. [
Data Ingestion
Data Ingestion involves gathering, transferring, processing, transforming, and correlating various datasets to create a queryable, contextualized repository, at the appropriate level of granularity, accessibility, and completeness to support activities in all of the FinOps Capabilities across all FinOps Personas. Domain: Understand Usage & Cost](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/data-ingestion/) 7. [
Executive Strategy Alignment
Executive Strategy Alignment connects technology-related spend, usage and adoption to the organization's business strategy and priorities, helping leaders compare options, make tradeoffs, and govern investment for value. Domain: Manage the FinOps Practice](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/executive-strategy-alignment/) 8. [
FinOps Assessment
Assessment of the FinOps practice allows a FinOps team to measure its own effectiveness, map its activities against the goals of the organization, and identify areas where it will be valuable to mature those activities. Domain: Manage the FinOps Practice](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/finops-assessment/) 9. [
FinOps Education & Enablement
FinOps Education & Enablement allows everyone participating in a FinOps practice to develop a common understanding of FinOps concepts, terminology, and practice. Domain: Manage the FinOps Practice](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/finops-education-enablement/) 10. [
FinOps Practice Operations
FinOps Practice Operations encompasses the set of activities required to build and operate a FinOps practice within an organization. Domain: Manage the FinOps Practice](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/finops-practice-operations/) 11. [
Forecasting
Forecasting involves creating a model of the anticipated future cost and value of systems leveraging statistical methods, historical spend patterns, planned changes, and related metrics. Domain: Quantify Business Value](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/forecasting/) 12. [
Governance, Policy & Risk
Establishing and evolving policies, controls and governance mechanisms to ensure that technology use aligns with business objectives, complies with regulatory requirements, and optimizes technology resources efficiently. Domain: Manage the FinOps Practice](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/governance-policy-risk/) 13. [
Intersecting Disciplines
Coordinating activities with interconnected disciplines or Allied Persona teams that collaborate with FinOps teams. Domain: Manage the FinOps Practice](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/intersecting-disciplines/) 14. [
Invoicing & Chargeback
Developing specific reporting, service provider invoice reconciliation workflows and chargeback models, in direct collaboration with Finance Personas with input from Product and Engineering Personas, to align technology cost data and reporting to specific budgets and accounting requirements. Domain: Manage the FinOps Practice](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/invoicing-chargeback/) 15. [
KPIs & Benchmarking
KPIs & Benchmarking allows organizations to compare unit metrics and KPIs for important aspects of cloud value and optimization both internally between different teams, and externally with other organizations using cloud in similar ways. Domain: Quantify Business Value](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/kpis-benchmarking/) 16. [
Licensing & SaaS
Understanding and optimizing the impact of software licenses and SaaS investments on an organization's technology value structure and value. Domain: Optimize Usage & Cost](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/licensing-saas/) 17. [
Planning & Estimating
Estimation and exploration of potential cost and value of workloads if implemented across an organization's infrastructure and environments in a particular model or models. Domain: Quantify Business Value](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/planning-estimating/) 18. [
Rate Optimization
Driving rate efficiency through a combination of strategic vendor use, negotiated discounts, commitment discounts, and other pricing mechanisms to meet the organization's strategic, operational and budgetary objectives. Domain: Optimize Usage & Cost](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/rate-optimization/) 19. [
Reporting & Analytics
Examine and showback technology data to gain insights into usage & spend patterns to identify cost optimization opportunities, improve cost efficiency, and make informed financial decisions regarding resources. Domain: Understand Usage & Cost](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/reporting-analytics/) 20. [
Sustainability
Sustainability defines how the organization will make decisions about using technology in ways that consider both its impact on the environment and the organization's broader sustainability goals. Domain: Optimize Usage & Cost](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/sustainability/) 21. [
Unit Economics
Develop and track metrics that provide an understanding of how an organization's technology use and management practices impact the value of the organization's products, services, or activities. Domain: Quantify Business Value](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/unit-economics/) 22. [
Usage Optimization
Analyze and optimize technology resources to match specific usage patterns while ensuring that workloads operate efficiently and generate sufficient business value for their cost. Domain: Optimize Usage & Cost](https://www.finops.org/framework/capabilities/usage-optimization/)
