WHAT DO LEADERS USE TEAM TOPOLOGIES FOR?
Team-of-teams design for product-driven and platform-driven organizations (aka project-to-product transformation, adopting product operating model).
Scale an existing product organization without compromising the speed of value and pace of innovation.
Scale the adoption of ** Cloud and accelerate value creation with complex cloud native architectures.**
Streamline organizational design to accelerate flow of value.
Build Platform-as-a-Product and/or Platform Engineering strategy and corresponding team-of-teams design to execute it.
Accelerating adoption and value creation of new technologies (e.g. AI/ML), without disrupting the flow of value or introducing complexity in your architectures.
Our team of practitioners brings together deep knowledge of team-of-teams design through Team Topologies combined with other methods and ideas (Agile, DevOps, Value Stream Management, Domain-Driven Design, Wardley Mapping).

WHERE SHOULD YOU START IN YOUR ORGANIZATION?
TEAM TOPOLOGIES Assessment
Assess different areas of your organization for fast flow of value and get guidance on where and how to start.
The assessment covers the 10 key dimensions of organizational agility. These act as prerequisites for the adoption of Team Topologies and other principles that enable fast flow of value. They show where your organization is in terms of being flow-oriented or hindered by organizational processes.
Our team of coaches and practitioners also offer a deeper dive with your team/s in an Expert Review & Assessment. It takes a few weeks and covers your architecture/s, SDLC processes and team-of-teams organization. Get in touch if you are interested in this engagement.
